About us

Food in a Box is an eclectic restaurant on Barreda St in Bislig City, Surigao del Sur. Rice boxes, silog breakfasts, sizzling plates, pasta, burgers, café drinks and more in a cozy, casual space. With meals at ₱1–500 per person, it's a friendly spot for breakfast, lunch, dinner or a quick coffee. Dine in, take out or have it delivered.
